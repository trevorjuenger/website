Academic leadership profile

Film educator, equipment manager, and independent filmmaker with more than ten years of higher-education teaching experience at Southwestern Illinois College and Webster University. Experienced in curriculum development, course scheduling, program building, student assessment, faculty recruitment, budgeting, purchasing, equipment inventory, student advising, and regional arts and industry partnerships. Teaching spans production, cinematography, post-production, motion graphics, visual effects, film history, film appreciation, and artificial intelligence in filmmaking.

Academic and program leadership

  • Serve as Equipment Manager for the Southwestern Illinois College Film Program, overseeing an inventory of more than 200 production assets.
  • Administered $6,600 of the Film Program's $9,800 equipment budget for 2026–27, prioritizing camera and lighting equipment, software, and maintenance.
  • Recruited prospective industry members and began organizing a Film Program advisory committee.
  • Initiated and submitted an articulation-agreement proposal between Southwestern Illinois College and Webster University.
  • Helped develop the initial core-course schedule following the merger of Webster University's film and video departments.
  • Founded SWIC's first Cinema Club, developed a regional filmmaker speaker series with SWICArts, and organized the first Southwestern Illinois Film Festival.
  • Advise students on degree completion, transfer planning, continuing education, and professional development.

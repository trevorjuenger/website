About

DIY cinema as a way of working.

Trevor Juenger is an independent filmmaker and educator from rural Illinois. His work moves between horror, surrealism, fractured narrative, photography, and video installation.

He champions ultra-low-budget filmmaking for the creative freedom it gives artists working outside traditional financing. His features and shorts have screened internationally, received regional arts support, and earned festival recognition.

Teaching & leadership

Trevor teaches production, cinematography, editing, color, motion graphics, visual effects, film history, and emerging AI workflows at Southwestern Illinois College and Webster University.

2026–present

Equipment Manager · Southwestern Illinois College

Manages more than 200 production assets and leads research, purchasing, organization, maintenance, and student/faculty equipment support.

2016–present

Adjunct Instructor · Southwestern Illinois College

Production, cinematography, advanced post-production, film history, and film appreciation. Founded the Cinema Club, developed a filmmaker speaker series, and curated the first Southwestern Illinois Film Festival.

2016–present

Adjunct Instructor · Webster University

Undergraduate and graduate instruction in editing, color, motion graphics, visual effects, horror production, and artificial intelligence in filmmaking.

Program work

Curriculum, pathways, and faculty development

Helped schedule Webster's merged film/video core, recruited instructors hired by both colleges, convened a SWIC advisory committee, and initiated a proposed SWIC–Webster articulation agreement.

Courses and areas of instruction

Webster University: Edit and Color; Advanced Edit and Color; Graphics and Effects; Pre-production Senior Overview; Core Production; Micro-budget Feature Filmmaking; Adobe Premiere Certification; AI Advancements in Video Production; Advanced Editing; Color Grading by the Numbers; Professional Development; Horror Film Production; 360-degree Imaging.

Southwestern Illinois College: Film Appreciation; Film History; Movie Making I and II; Advanced Post-production; Video Graphics; Advanced Graphics and Animation; Cinematography; Advanced Cinematography; Chroma Key.

Software and systems: DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere, After Effects, Photoshop, Character Animator, Final Cut Pro X, SpeedGrade, Mocha, Final Draft, and Blackmagic, Canon, DJI, Sony, RED, and Panasonic camera systems.
